# :seedling: Welcome to CityGreen:seedling: 

## :sunglasses: Who are we?

CityGreen is a project team building **full-chain urban unmanned farms driven by swarm intelligence**. We are incubated at the Computational Intelligence Lab — MetaEvolution Lab (MetaEvo), School of Computer Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology (SCUT), and supported by Panorama Optimization (Guangzhou) AI Technology Co., Ltd.

We are not trying to build one "do-everything" agricultural robot. Instead, we organize a group of low-cost, small and modular unmanned devices — UAVs, UGVs, IoT sensors and smart irrigation units — into an **edge operating swarm**, and pair it with a **cloud-side collective intelligence** that perceives, plans, schedules and keeps learning. The two form a closed loop: *cloud decision → edge collaboration → field operation → data feedback → continuous optimization*. A single device has limited capability, yet the swarm together can take on complex farming work, and the system can be reconfigured plot by plot as the scenario changes.

The team follows a three-tier pyramid: an **academic advisory** layer of professors, a **project core** layer of postdocs and PhD students, and a **technical collaboration** layer of more than 10 master's students and over 20 undergraduates working in six parallel engineering teams. We keep the tradition of "seniors guiding juniors, students growing inside real research tasks", aiming to give more urban land and urban space an **AI farm brain**.



## :hammer_and_wrench: What we work on

- Swarm intelligence and multi-agent collaboration
- Meta-black-box optimization (MetaBBO) and automated algorithm design
- Reinforcement learning and evolutionary computation
- World models and digital twins for agricultural environments
- UAV / UGV path planning, task allocation and cross-plot scheduling
- LLM agents and MLOps pipelines for field deployment
- Multi-source data fusion for crop, soil, weather and equipment states

| Layer                   | Who                                                          | What we do                                                   |

| Technical collaboration | 10+ master's students and 20+ undergraduates                 | Six parallel engineering teams covering the whole technical chain |

The six teams running in parallel:

- **Core Agent team** — LLM-based decision agents, task decomposition and agent workflows.
- **Algorithm team** — optimization and scheduling algorithms, MetaBBO solvers.
- **UAV flight control team** — aerial platforms, inspection and low-altitude operation.
- **UGV team** — ground platforms, transport and obstacle avoidance.
- **UE world model team** — digital twin, simulation environment and the virtual-real closed loop.
- **UAV swarm coordination team** — formation control and multi-UAV cooperative operation.
